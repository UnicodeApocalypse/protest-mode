Protest Mode helps you check and set up your phone to limit the risks when you take part in protests.

<b>Clear, quick overview</b>
Check your phone's privacy at a glance. The risk is shown as a level, with simple and understandable categories.

<b>Passive</b>
The app changes nothing without your consent: it checks the configuration and guides you to adjust it yourself.

<b>Private</b>
No Internet connection, no trackers and no data collection. Developed as open source.
